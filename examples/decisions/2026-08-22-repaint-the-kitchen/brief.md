# Repaint the kitchen this autumn, or leave it until spring?

*An invented example. Alex is not a real person.*

**The question.** The kitchen walls are scuffed. Repaint this autumn, or wait until spring?

**Can it be undone?** Yes. A two-way door, and a small one.

**How much is at stake?** Low.

**Where it stands.** Turned back. While framing it, Alex said the kitchen is fine for now and the paint shop's sale is
in March. There was nothing to decide yet, so Bryn closed it rather than run anything.
