# Take the Tuesday evening pottery class or the Saturday morning one?

*An invented example. Alex is not a real person.*

**The question.** Two places left in a ten-week beginners' pottery course: Tuesday 7 p.m. or Saturday 10 a.m.

**Can it be undone?** Yes. The studio lets you switch after the first month. A two-way door.

**How much is at stake?** Low.

**What Bryn checked first.** The cairn has a standing marker for this: POL-003, hobby sign-ups for a term go to the
slot you can reach without driving. The Tuesday studio is on the bus route.

**What Bryn said.** "This is a small call you can undo, and you already have a marker for it. No council needed. Pick
Tuesday, and switch after the first month if it does not suit."
