# Brainstorm: host the family holiday dinner?

*An invented example. Session 2026-10-03.*

## Diverge

**Alex's seeds first.** Host as usual. Ask a cousin. Everyone brings a dish.

**Round 1 (free-wheel, quota 10).** Host, but start at 2 p.m. so it ends early. Book the back room at the community
hall. Two smaller dinners on two days. A walk first, dinner after. Order the main and cook the sides. Swap years with
a cousin. A breakfast instead of a dinner. Ask the teenagers to run dessert. One big shopping trip with two helpers.
Skip a year and say so early.

**Round 2 (constraint shuffle: "no cooking at all").** A picnic in the park if it is mild. Order everything. A
restaurant with a set menu. Each household brings one course, fully cooked.

**Round 3 (reversal: "how would this go badly for sure?").** Say yes, plan nothing, shop the day before, cook alone.
Inverted: a written plan by mid-November, a helper per course, the shopping done in two trips.

## Converge

**Clusters.** Host as usual (3) · share the cooking (5) · move it out of the house (4) · change the shape (4) ·
skip or swap (2).

**Triage (New, Useful, Feasible).** Nine candidates down to four.

**Criteria (Alex's, weighted).** Rest for Alex ×2 · everyone together ×2 · cost ×1.

| Candidate | Rest | Together | Cost | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Host, but everyone brings a dish | good | yes | low | 9 | Keeps the tradition, splits the load |
| Book the back room at the community hall | good | yes | medium | 8 | Room for twelve, no tidy-up at home |
| Ask a cousin to host and bring dessert | best | yes | low | 8 | Fair, but it is Alex's turn |
| Host at home for twelve, as usual | poor | yes | medium | 5 | The tradition, at the cost of rest |

**Shortlist for the council.** All four, because the top three are close and the tie turns on family feeling, which the
criteria do not capture. That is a council question, not a scoring one.
