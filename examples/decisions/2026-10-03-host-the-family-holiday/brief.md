# Host the family holiday dinner this year, or ask someone else?

*An invented example. Alex is not a real person.*

**The question.** The family takes turns hosting the holiday dinner. It is Alex's turn: twelve people, a small dining
room, and a new job that started in September. Host, or ask someone else this year?

**Can it be undone?** Yes, until invitations go out in three weeks. A two-way door, but with high stakes for family
feeling, so Bryn suggested the full council and Alex agreed.

**How much is at stake?** High, in family terms. Low in money.

**What a good outcome looks like.** Everyone fed and glad they came, and Alex not exhausted in the first months of a
new job.

**What Bryn checked first.** Nothing in the cairn about hosting. No earlier calls of this shape.

**The cost, said before it ran.** A brainstorm on Sonnet, then a council of eleven model runs. Alex said yes.
