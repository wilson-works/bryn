# Premortem transcript: foster first, then adopt

*An invented example. Run 2026-09-28 19:30. Investigators on Sonnet, the synthesis on Opus.*

## The plan

Foster a calm younger dog for six weeks from December. If the two settle, adopt. If not, the dog goes back to the
rescue's foster network.

## The frame

It is six months from now. The plan failed. Why?

## Failure reasons (five investigated)

1. **The dogs never settled.** The first dog guarded the sofa and the food bowl from day two. Walks became two separate
   walks. *The assumption:* that a calm dog would be calm with this dog. *Early warning:* stiff posture at the bowl in
   the first three days.
2. **The goodbye was too hard.** Six weeks was long enough to get attached, so the dog was adopted anyway when it should
   not have been. *The assumption:* that the household would decide on the evidence. *Early warning:* talking about
   "when" instead of "if" in week two.
3. **A vet bill landed in the same month.** The older dog needed dental work in January. *The assumption:* that the
   older dog is healthy. *Early warning:* skipping the annual check because "she's fine".
4. **Winter walks broke the routine.** Two dogs in the dark before work meant shorter walks for both. *The assumption:*
   that walks scale. *Early warning:* walks under twenty minutes two days running.
5. **Office days.** The three office days left two dogs alone for nine hours. *The assumption:* that company replaces a
   midday break. *Early warning:* signs of chewing or barking reported by a neighbour.

## The synthesis

**The most likely failure.** The two dogs never settle, and walks become two separate walks.

**The most dangerous failure.** A long vet bill for the older dog lands in the same month as the adoption costs.

**The hidden assumption.** That the first dog wants company. Nobody has checked that yet.

**The revised plan.**
- Before fostering: the older dog's annual check, including teeth.
- Before fostering: a weekend with a friend's calm dog, watched closely.
- During fostering: decide at week four, on a written list of three signs, before attachment decides for you.
- Book a midday dog walker for office days during the trial.

**The pre-start checklist.** The vet check is done. The friend's-dog weekend went well. The three signs are written
down. The midday walker is booked.
