# Adopt a second dog this winter, or wait a year?

*An invented example. Alex is not a real person.*

**The question.** Alex has one dog, a seven-year-old who gets anxious when the house is empty. A local rescue has a
younger dog that would fit. Adopt this winter, or wait a year?

**The options.**
1. Adopt this winter.
2. Wait a year.
3. Foster first, then decide.

**Can it be undone?** Not really. Returning an adopted dog is possible, but it is hard on the dog and on the household.
A one-way door, so Bryn will never turn this into a standing rule. It gets the full council, and a premortem on the
plan the council favours.

**How much is at stake?** High: two animals' wellbeing, a few years of walks, and vet bills.

**What a good outcome looks like.** Two settled dogs, a calmer first dog when the house is empty, and walks that fit
around work.

**The cost, said before it ran.** The council is eleven model runs; the premortem up to six more (five investigators
and a synthesis). Alex said yes to both.
