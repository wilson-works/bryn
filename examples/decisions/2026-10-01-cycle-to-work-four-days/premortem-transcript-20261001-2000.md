# Premortem transcript: cycle to work four days a week

*An invented example. Run 2026-10-01 20:00. Investigators on Sonnet, the synthesis on Opus.*

## The frame

It is six months from now. The plan to cycle four days a week failed. Why?

## Failure reasons (four investigated)

1. **The first heavy rain broke the habit.** Week three brought four wet days. Each morning was a fresh decision at
   7 a.m., and each time the car won. It never restarted. *Early warning:* deciding the weather question on the
   doorstep.
2. **The junction by the bridge.** No bike lane, and after the clocks changed it was dark at going-home time. One near
   miss ended the plan. *Early warning:* the first ride home after the clocks change.
3. **Nowhere to change.** Arriving damp three days in a row, with no shower at work. *Early warning:* carrying a second
   shirt "just in case" in week one.
4. **Four days was too many to start.** Legs and the week both needed a ramp. *Early warning:* skipping a day by
   Thursday of week one.

## The synthesis

**The most likely failure.** The first week of heavy rain breaks the habit, and it never restarts.

**The most dangerous failure.** The busy junction by the bridge with no bike lane, in the dark after the clocks change.

**The hidden assumption.** That there is a shower or somewhere to change at work.

**The revised plan.** Start with two days for a month. Decide the bad-weather rule now, not at 7 in the morning: on a
rain or ice warning, take the bus. Use the quiet route over the footbridge, which avoids the junction. Ask the office
about the shower before day one.

**The pre-start checklist.** Ride the route once on Sunday and time it. Lights charged. The bus times saved. The shower
question asked.
