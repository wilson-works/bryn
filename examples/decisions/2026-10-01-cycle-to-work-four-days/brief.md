# Cycle to work four days a week instead of driving?

*An invented example. Alex is not a real person.*

**The question.** Alex already has a plan: cycle to work four days a week from next Monday, drive one. Is the plan
sound? This is a plan, not a choice between options, so Bryn skipped the council and went straight to the premortem.

**Can it be undone?** Yes. A two-way door.

**How much is at stake?** Low in money. The real stake is safety at one junction, so the premortem looked hardest there.

**What a good outcome looks like.** Still cycling most weeks in March, with no near misses.

**The cost, said before it ran.** A premortem is up to six model runs. Alex said yes.
