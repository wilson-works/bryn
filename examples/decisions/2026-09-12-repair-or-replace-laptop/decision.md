# The decision: repair the laptop

*An invented example, in Alex's words, 2026-09-13.*

"Repairing it. The shop quoted a third of the price of a refurbished one and said the hinge and screen are fine.
The photos are on the external drive now. Looking again in March."

**Filed in the cairn as** POL-001: repair things used every day before replacing them, when the repair is under a
third of the price of a replacement. This was the third call of the same shape (the washing machine in March, the bike
in June), so it became a standing marker.

**Look again on** 2027-03-01.
