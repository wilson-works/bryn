# Brainstorm: a garden plot or raised beds?

*An invented example. Session 2026-08-30.*

## Diverge

**Alex's seeds.** A plot at the community garden. Raised beds at home.

**Round 1 (free-wheel, quota 8).** Containers on the patio first. Share a plot with the neighbours. One raised bed,
not two. Herbs on the windowsill only. Join the garden's shared beds as a volunteer. A plot plus a compost bin at home.

decide in a year · share a plot with the neighbours.
