# Take a community garden plot next year, or build raised beds at home?

*An invented example. Alex is not a real person.*

**The question.** Grow vegetables next year: a plot at the community garden ten minutes away, or two raised beds by
the back fence?

**How much is at stake?** Low.

**Where it stands.** Parked. The garden's waiting list for next year does not open until February, so the question
cannot be settled now. Bryn set a date to look again rather than run a council on a guess.
