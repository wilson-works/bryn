# Foster for six weeks before adopting a second dog

*An invented example. Alex has not decided yet; this record is proposed.*

- **Status:** proposed

## Context

The first dog is anxious when the house is empty. A second dog might help or might add a second worry, and nobody
knows which for this dog. Adoption is hard to undo, so this is a one-way door: it is recorded here every time and never
becomes a standing marker.

## Decision

Foster a calm younger dog for six weeks from December, decide at week four on three written signs, and adopt only if
both dogs have settled.

## Consequences

Good: the real unknown gets tested before anything is permanent. Bad: six weeks is long enough to get attached, which
can push the decision; the three written signs are there to stop that. A midday walker costs money during the trial.

## Confirmation

The vet check, the friend's-dog weekend, the three signs on paper and the walker booked, before the foster dog arrives.
