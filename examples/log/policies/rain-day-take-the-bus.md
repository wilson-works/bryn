# POL-002: On a rain or ice warning, take the bus  (v1, active)

*An invented example.*

- **Trigger:** a weather warning for rain or ice on a planned cycling day
- **Rule:** take the bus; no guilt, and no deciding it again at the door
- **Triage basis:** recurring (most weeks in winter), reversible (it is one day), stakes bounded (a bus fare)
- **Escalate-if:** three bus days in a row (look at the whole plan again) · the bus route changes
- **Provenance:** premortem 2026-10-01 (cycling to work); the most likely failure was the morning weather decision
- **Status:** active · **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02
