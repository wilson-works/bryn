# POL-003: Hobby classes go to the slot you can reach without driving  (v1, active)

*An invented example.*

- **Trigger:** signing up for a hobby class or club for a term, with more than one time slot
- **Rule:** pick the slot you can reach on foot, by bike or by bus; switch after the first month if it does not suit
- **Triage basis:** recurring (choir, swimming lessons, now pottery), reversible (most places let you switch), stakes bounded (one term)
- **Escalate-if:** the class costs more than a month of the household's fun budget · it clashes with a standing family commitment
- **Provenance:** written after the third sign-up of the same shape; applied to the pottery class on 2026-09-19 without a council
- **Status:** active · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-19
