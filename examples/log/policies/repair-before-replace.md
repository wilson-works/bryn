# POL-001: Repair things used every day before replacing them  (v1, active)

*An invented example.*

- **Trigger:** something used every day breaks or wears out (a laptop, a washing machine, a bike)
- **Rule:** get a written repair quote first; repair when it is under a third of the price of a replacement, and set a date to look again
- **Triage basis:** recurring (three times this year), reversible (a repaired thing can still be replaced), stakes bounded (the worst case is one repair bill)
- **Escalate-if:** the same thing broke twice in a year · anything to do with safety (gas, brakes, wiring) · the repair carries no guarantee
- **Provenance:** council 2026-09-12 (the laptop), after the washing machine in March and the bike in June went the same way
- **Status:** active · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-13
