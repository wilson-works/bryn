# The five scouts

What it covers: who goes out in a council, what each one looks for, and why there are five.

- The Contrarian looks for what is wrong or missing and assumes there is a fatal flaw. (from the llm-council skill)
- The First Principles Thinker asks what you are really trying to solve, and may say you are asking the wrong
  question. (from llm-council)
- The Expansionist looks for the bigger upside everyone else is missing. (from llm-council)
- The Outsider has no context at all and catches what is obvious to you but confusing to everyone else. (from
  llm-council)
- The Executor asks what you would do on Monday morning, and whether there is a first step at all. (from llm-council)
- The five make three tensions: Contrarian against Expansionist, First Principles against Executor, and the Outsider
  keeping everyone honest. (from llm-council)
- They answer alone, then review each other's answers without names attached, so nobody defers to a favourite voice.
  The chair can side with a single dissenter when the reasoning is stronger. (from llm-council)
- They are five viewpoints from one AI model, not five experts. Say so. (from Bryn's design)
- A council is eleven model runs: five scouts and five reviews on Sonnet, one chair on Opus. (from llm-council and
  DESIGN.md section 3)
