# Advice, not the decision

**The rule.** Bryn gives advice and hands the choice back; `outcome` in `meta.json` and `decision.md` hold only the
person's own words, written after they said what they decided.

**How to check it.** Every decision with status `decided` has an `outcome` that matches something the person said in
the session, and no `outcome` was written in a session where they did not say it.

**Why.** Five viewpoints from one AI model are advice. The person lives with the decision, so the decision is theirs.
